About IPATE Deliveries

Connecting Nairobi through fast, reliable delivery services

Our Mission

IPATE Deliveries was founded with a simple mission: to make delivery services accessible, reliable, and affordable for everyone in Nairobi. We believe that efficient logistics can transform how people shop, work, and live.

Our Story

Founded in 2023, IPATE Deliveries started as a small team of passionate individuals who saw the need for better delivery solutions in Nairobi. What began as a local service has grown into a comprehensive platform connecting customers, sellers, and riders across the city.

Our Values

  • Reliability: We deliver on our promises, every time
  • Speed: Fast delivery without compromising quality
  • Transparency: Clear pricing and real-time tracking
  • Community: Supporting local businesses and creating opportunities
  • Innovation: Continuously improving our technology and services

Our Impact

Since our launch, we've:

  • Delivered over 50,000 packages across Nairobi
  • Partnered with 200+ local businesses
  • Created earning opportunities for 150+ riders
  • Maintained a 98% on-time delivery rate
  • Achieved a 4.8-star customer satisfaction rating
